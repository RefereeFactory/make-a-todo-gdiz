    How it works

    1. Add anything — type a task, choose a priority, press Add.
    2. Tick it off — tap the circle when it's done; it moves to your Done list.
    3. Everything is remembered — your list is saved to the cloud automatically, so it's waiting for you every time you return, on any device.
    4. Filter — switch between All, To do and Done, and clear completed tasks when you like.
